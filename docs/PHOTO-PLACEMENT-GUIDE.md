# Photo placement guide — Erika Page website

> **These picks are live on the site.** 65 slots ship with their photo already in place
> (`assets/photos/`, declared in `photos.php`, wired as each field's default in `fields.php`).
> In the admin, every one of those slots shows a **photo library** strip — click A2, A3, A4…
> and Save to swap, or upload your own as before. Two deliberate exceptions to "always A1":
> the no-background slot on the home page uses **02/A6**, the only true transparent cut-out
> (A1 has a white background that would show as a white box on the rose panel), and the
> Atlanta skyline slots use **11/A2**, because 11/A1 is only 682 px wide.

## Three things to check before publishing

**1. The AI-generated images (folder 15).** Seven "Real Estate Keynote" images plus the pink-suit
portrait were generated by AI, and the woman in them does not look like Erika. One is a fake
magazine cover with garbled text. I did not place any of them. Three more AI images *do* match her
likeness and are placed but clearly labelled: `01/A6`, `07/A1`, `10/A5` — keep or drop them as a
brand decision, but don't present them as photography.

**2. Five picks are HEIC** (`08/A3`, `09/A5`, `09/A6`, `12/A6`, `14/A5`). The CMS only accepts
JPG/PNG/WEBP/GIF, and browsers can't display HEIC — export them as JPG before upload. They are
named `CONVERT FROM HEIC` so they're easy to spot.

**3. Resolution.** Most picks are 1600 px or larger. Three are small and should only go in cards,
never a full-width hero: `08/A1` (800×800), `11/A1` (682×1024), and `01/A1` (1024×1536 — for a big
hero use `01/A2` instead, which is larger).

*One label to ignore:* `12/A6` is named "luxury vehicle detail" but is actually a private-aircraft
cabin shot. It still belongs in that folder.
