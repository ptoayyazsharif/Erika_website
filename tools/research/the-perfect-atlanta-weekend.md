# Research notes: the perfect Atlanta weekend

Article: `/blog/the-perfect-atlanta-weekend` (short link `/weekend`).
Podcast script: `tools/podcast/the-perfect-atlanta-weekend.txt`.
Researched 16 October 2026. Every fact in the article and the script is listed here with its
source and the words in it. Dated items are starred (★).

The spine is her own Atlanta-weekend material, all unused until now: the video
`erika-explains-perfect-atlanta-weekend`, `atlanta-this-weekend`, `phipps-green-fridays`,
`atlantas-rooftops`, and the reels `DbMgF1chCj1`, `Da6I7GNh-2C`, `Dbe3bbbgb0c`,

**Re-checked against today:** the article keeps to places that are there all year
(Ponce City Market and its roof, the BeltLine, Buckhead, Madison). Her dated items are left
out: Buckhead Restaurant Week (July), Smorgasburg, a Braves–Nationals game ("Friday, July
thirty-first"), the Ponce cocktail class and "Placitas at Ponce", and the rooftop bar she
named as "one of Atlanta's newest". Phipps Plaza's Screen on the Green is kept, in her
words, with "check the current schedule": the only 2026 listing found was a third-party
calendar with an inconsistent date (Oct 3 isn't a Friday in 2026), and no official page
could be read.
