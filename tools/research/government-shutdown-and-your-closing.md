# Research notes: a government shutdown and your closing, explained

Article: `/blog/government-shutdown-and-your-closing` (short link `/shutdown`).
Podcast script: `tools/podcast/government-shutdown-and-your-closing.txt`.
Researched 30 October 2026. Every fact in the article and the script is listed here with its
source and the words in it. Dated items are starred (★).

The spine is her video **`raw-task-1-changes`** (`ceo_erika` `intake/transcripts/video/`, ~250
words; Drive id in `intake/catalogue/video-ids.tsv`). She made it during a government shutdown
("This government shutdown could really be impactful on your timeline"); its date isn't on file,
so the article says only "during a government shutdown". Her flood-insurance mention fits the
October–November 2025 lapse.

**Why now:** the stopgap funding bill keeps the government funded and the NFIP authorized only
through 11 December 2026 (sources 1–2), so December closings face the same risk.

Read with curl as a browser (HUD, VA, CRS, the bill) or WebFetch (IRS, NAHB). **USDA's own site
(usda.gov) answered 403 to curl and to WebFetch** for its Rural Development shutdown plans and its
FY2027 budget notes, so USDA is sourced from NAHB's October 2025 post, which quotes RD's terms.

## Deliberately left out

- **Her "FHA loan is the most common loan in the United States right now"**: no source read
  supports it, and HMDA data weren't read. Left out rather than "corrected".
- **Her "suspended in many zones"**: the NFIP doesn't suspend by zone; the article gives the
  precise rule (new policies can't be written; the requirement applies in SFHAs).
- **Whether H.R. 6500 was signed, and when**: a secondary site says it was signed 2 Sep 2026; not
  read at an official source. The article says "the bill Congress passed".
- **USDA's FY2027 proposal** to let guaranteed closings continue in a shutdown (seen in search
  results only; usda.gov refused every request).
- **Politics**: who is to blame for a shutdown, or how likely one is. Not our subject.
- **Her Escaluxe sign-off** ("elevate your lifestyle, escalate your moves, and enjoy Escaluxe").
