## Deliberately left out

- **The date DU started applying it** (15 or 16 Nov 2025 in trade press): the
  announcement PDF couldn't be read. The article says "announced November 5, 2025" (source 1).
- **FHFA's Bill Pulte quote** ("our underwriting standards are the same") — trade press only.
- **"Lenders may still set their own minimums"** as a stated fact: true in practice, but
  no primary source read says it. The article says what is in the Guide (DU decides
  eligibility for Fannie Mae; the lender makes the loan; manual and government loans keep
  620) and says "the change doesn't guarantee anybody a loan".
- **Freddie Mac's rules** — not read at the source.
- **Rates, payments, loan-level price adjustments by score** — the series has no numbers
  that advertise credit terms, and price adjustments by score would be exactly that.
- **Her mentoring-call method** (running the credit profile through the lender even for a
  lease-purchase client; `memory/domain/real-estate-craft.md`) — it comes from private
  mentoring calls, not a public recording, so it isn't quoted.
- **"helped luxury clients navigate these hurdles"** from E1 — "navigate" is on her
  never-use list for written copy; the clients line is quoted instead.
