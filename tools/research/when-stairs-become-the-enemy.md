## Deliberately left out

- **"We've got five coming to market in the next three months"**: a dated listing claim.
- **Hip-fracture and hospitalization counts** (CDC): true, but one figure too many for
  a short article.
- **Specific single-level home features** (no-step entries, door widths): no official
  source was read for them; the touring tip sticks to the CDC checklist's own areas.
- **County senior exemptions** (Gwinnett, Cobb, DeKalb…): not researched county by
  county; the article sends readers to their tax commissioner.
- **AARP survey figures** on wanting to age in place: not read at the source.
