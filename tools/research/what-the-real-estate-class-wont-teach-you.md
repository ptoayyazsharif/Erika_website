## Deliberately left out

- **"The average lead converts after 12 to 17 touches. The average agent quits after 2."** (E3 and
  its caption): no source for either figure. Her compliance note (`business/licensee-advertising-
  compliance`) says to cut a risky claim rather than caption it. Her lesson behind it (follow-up
  and consistency) is kept.
- **"AI Erika"** (the captions of E2, E3 and E1's "#Arive"): her AI follow-up product. Whether it
  is offered publicly, and on what terms, is waiting on her (`business/ai-twin-pilot`).
- **Fees.** GREC lists an examination fee and a license fee; the article says fees change and
  points to GREC instead (the no-prices rule, and they go stale).
- **Her bird-dog reel `DbyPKNCCNFJ`** was considered for today and **not used**: she says she
  compensates her bird dogs, and source 2 says a licensee may not pay a fee or give a gift for a
  referral to an unlicensed person. Flagged to the owner.
- **Broker licensing, reciprocity, community association managers:** not what a new agent needs.
