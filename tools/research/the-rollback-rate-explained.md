## Conflicts resolved at the source

- **Gwinnett's rollback rate: 6.792 or 6.782?** The 14 July release says 6.792; the
  county's online comment page says 6.782. The legal notice (source 2) says "no more than
  6.792 mills", and 6.950 − 0.158 = 6.792. Used 6.792.

## Deliberately left out

- **The notice's dollar examples** ("$28.44" on a $475,000 home): a home price, against
  her no-prices rule. They also read oddly next to the county's own Value Offset
  Exemption statement.
- **The school district's rates** (18.70 / 1.45 mills): seen only in a search summary.
- **A tax-bill disclaimer when the adopted rate exceeds the estimated roll-back rate.**
  Seen only in county summaries of HB 581 / HB 92, not read in the statute; left out.
- **Unincorporated Gwinnett's total 14.71 mills**: true (source 3), but a total that
  mixes service districts. Not needed.
- **Every other county's rates**: the article uses Gwinnett as the example, as she did.
