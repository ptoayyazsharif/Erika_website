# Research notes: renting or buying sight unseen, explained

Article: `/blog/sight-unseen` (short link `/sight-unseen`).
Podcast script: `tools/podcast/sight-unseen.txt`.
Researched 28 October 2026. Every fact in the article and the script is listed here with its
source and the words in it. Dated items are starred (★).

The spine is her video **"Respect your time"**, part 1 of 2 (`Video_Edting_Erika/projects/
respect-your-time`, edited 21 Sep 2026; transcript in `ceo_erika` `intake/edits/respect-your-time.md`;
also `memory/domain/virtual-tours`). It is a talk about a mentee who planned virtual tours for two
clients. **Only her general lines about virtual tours are used.** The mentee and the two clients
aren't described, and the video's real subject (respecting an agent's own time) is left for a
coaching piece. She says "twenty-six years" on camera; the written article says "24+ years" (the
brand rule). The spoken script says neither.

The scam facts are from the FTC's one-page handout (consumer.ftc.gov's HTML articles answered 404
to curl; the PDF downloaded and was read with pypdf) and from Georgia's Attorney General's
Consumer Protection Division (read as HTML, 28 Oct).
