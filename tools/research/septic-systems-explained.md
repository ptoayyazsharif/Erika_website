## Deliberately left out

- **The property manager's lines and her reaction to them** ("is this your listing?", "Life in the
  big city"): a private call with an identifiable third party.
- **Her siting details** ("level ground, flat … front yard, … backyard, … side yard", the field-line
  length limit): the manual has detailed site rules, too technical to summarise safely; the
  article says the county permit decides.
- **"The drawing on the county website" as a fact**: kept as her tip in her words; which counties
  publish septic drawings online wasn't checked.
- **Occupancy laws**: she promises another video on them; not researched here.
- **The manual's pumping note** ("about once every three to five years"): the EPA's wording is used
  instead, as the current consumer guidance.
