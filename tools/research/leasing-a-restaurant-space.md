## Deliberately left out

- **The City of Atlanta's certificate-of-occupancy rules.** atlantaga.gov refuses every
  automated request, so its own wording couldn't be read. Gwinnett's is used as the
  example, and readers are told to ask their city or county.
- **The Georgia DPH food code FAQ** (dph.georgia.gov answers 403). The county health
  departments' documents are used instead; they apply the same state rules (Chapter
  511-6-1, named in the Fulton brochure).
- **Fees, rents and timelines in days.** These are prices or vary by jurisdiction. Her
  "$4,000 a month cheaper" is given as "thousands a month".
- **Alcohol licensing details.** Only "liquor license (if applicable)" is used, from
  the Fulton brochure.
- **Lease-term definitions from commercial sites** (NNN, CAM and so on). No official
  source found. The article uses her own list of what to negotiate and tells readers to
  have an attorney review the lease.
