## Deliberately left out

- Home prices, rents and any market figure (her rule: no prices, no undated market
  numbers).
- Rankings ("best suburbs", "best schools"). They are opinions and they date. The
  article points to the official report cards instead.
- The total count of Georgia school districts (181) and of city systems (21). The only
  sources found were a search snippet, an 11Alive story that blocks every fetch, and a
  Department of Education page with no readable body. None could be verified.
- The previous pollen record. Reports disagree (9,368 vs 9,369), and it isn't needed.
- The regional transit authority's commuter buses. Its site blocks every request, so
  nothing about them could be checked.
