# Research notes: the Wall Street homebuying ban, explained

Article: `/blog/wall-street-homebuying-ban-explained` (short link `/wall-street`).
Podcast script: `tools/podcast/wall-street-homebuying-ban-explained.txt`.
Researched 14 October 2026. Every fact in the article and the script is listed here with its
source and the words in it. Dated items are starred (★).

The spine is her own reel/video `banning-wall-street-homebuying` (161 words, early 2026),
unused until now. **Re-checked against today:** what she described as a proposal became
federal law on 11 July 2026 (the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act, H.R. 6644, Title X,
Sec. 1001), and the purchase ban takes effect on 7 January 2027. Her video's date isn't
recorded; the article says "earlier this year", which is true whichever month it was.

## Deliberately left out

- **Any claim of what it will do to prices** in Metro Atlanta: nobody can know yet. The
  article gives her prediction (attributed) and the Urban Institute's caution side by side.
- **The Treasury definitions under the executive order** — no primary source found; the law's
  definition now governs, and Treasury "may issue regulations" under it.
- **"Build-to-rent homes must be sold after seven years"** (Atlanta REALTORS, pre-enactment):
  not in the enrolled text.
- **Names of investor companies** and their Atlanta holdings — not needed, and no primary
  source read.
- **Political framing.** The article states what was signed, what passed and what the law
  says, nothing about who deserves credit.
