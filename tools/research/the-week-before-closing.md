## Deliberately left out

- **The client's name and street** (she says both on camera; the caption names her). A private
  person; the article doesn't need them.
- **The CFPB scam figures** ("risen 1,100 percent between 2015 and 2017", "nearly $1 billion" in
  2017): the post is archived and the numbers are years old.
- **Who conducts a Georgia closing** (a closing attorney): the closing-costs article already covers
  the closing attorney, and no official page saying so could be read today.
- **Timing of the walkthrough** (24–48 hours, the morning of): only commercial sources say, and they
  disagree.
