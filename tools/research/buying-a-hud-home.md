source and the words in it. Dated items are starred (★).

## Deliberately left out

- **Fannie Mae HomePath and its "First Look" period.** Every Fannie Mae page and guide
  answered 403 to curl and WebFetch, or needed a browser (homepath.fanniemae.com). Search
  results put First Look at 20 days (30 in Nevada) from 2014, but nothing current could be
  read, so it isn't in the article.
- **"HUD sells homes as is"**, as HUD's words: not found in the pages read. The article says
  what HUD does say (the IN/IE/UI repair codes) and advises an inspection as general advice.
- **Any claim about her client's home** (price, type of foreclosure, area): the caption gives
  none.
- **Georgia's foreclosure process**: not needed for buying a HUD home; no Georgia source read.
- **The client testimonial reel `Dce4h7rJ52a`** ("My hand was held…"): a different client,
  not linked to this one; not used.
