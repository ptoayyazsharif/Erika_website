## For the owner: RESPA and "benefits from NEXA"

CFPB Regulation X §1024.14(b): "No person shall give and no person shall accept any fee,
kickback or other thing of value" for referring settlement business, and a "thing of value"
is broadly defined (money, discounts, commissions…). §1024.15 allows affiliated business
arrangements only with a written disclosure and without requiring the consumer to use the
affiliate. An article recommending her lender is fine; being paid or rewarded per referral
is not, unless it fits an exception. Check any arrangement with Axen Realty's broker or an
attorney before accepting anything from NEXA.
