# Research notes: listing photos, editing, and drone shots

Article: `/blog/listing-photos-and-drones` (short link `/listing-photos`).
Podcast script: `tools/podcast/listing-photos-and-drones.txt`.
Researched 20 October 2026. Every fact in the article and the script is listed here with its
source and the words in it. Dated items are starred (★).

The spine is her reel `DcKdzUiulyk` with her photographer, Jonathan Martinez, flying a drone
over a listing of "a little over an acre". Her caption and her spoken lines are quoted; the
reel's closing claims ("over 25,000 buyers in our database", "your home is probably already
sold") are left out: no file supports them, and her accuracy rule says to claim only what she
can show.

## Deliberately left out

- **A percentage of buyers who rate photos "very useful".** Secondary write-ups of NAR's 2025
  Profile give 81%, 83% and (from an older year) 87%; the full report is members-only and
  could not be read. The article says what the readable summary says instead.
- **Her database figure and "your home is probably already sold"** (end of the reel): no
  supporting file, and a sale isn't hers to promise.
- **Virtual staging disclosure rules, MLS photo rules, and Georgia-specific advertising rules:**
  no official text was read for them today.
- **Airspace near Atlanta's airports** (LAANC authorizations): the FAA page read mentions
  airspace authorizations only for night flights in controlled airspace; the article doesn't
  go further than "ask the pilot".
- **The address and details of the listing in the reel:** the reel doesn't give them.
