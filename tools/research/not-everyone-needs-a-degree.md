## Deliberately left out

- **Which trades are on the HOPE Career Grant list.** Sources disagree on the count (12 on
  TCSG's page, 18 in TCSG handouts, 17 in a 2017 release) and no current official list
  could be read. The article tells readers to ask their technical college.
- **"Home Depot matches you with real employers"** as a stated fact: Home Depot's pages
  describe the Path to Pro Network job database, not matching. Her line is attributed;
  the article describes the network in Home Depot's own words.
- **The trade-school and $75k–150k "six figures once independent" claim** as fact; given
  as hers, with BLS medians beside it.
- **Georgia's contractor licensing rules**: sos.ga.gov answers 403. The article uses the
  BLS line that most states license electricians and plumbers and says to check before
  you work.
- **"after twenty-five years in real estate"** (part 1): the brand rule is 24+; not used.
- **Her sign-offs** ("Escalux…"), the profanity in part 2, and the "free gift / guide in
  my bio" (no such guide on the site).
