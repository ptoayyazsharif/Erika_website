## Deliberately left out

- **Her free checklist** (reel and caption: "Comment GROUND and I'll send you my free checklist"):
  not in her files. Nothing offered in its place; the owner is told a guide could follow if
  she supplies her checklist.
- **Which pharmacies or banks in Atlanta sit on ground leases:** no readable source names one.
  Her line is quoted as hers, as a general example.
- **One Atlantic Center's ground rent and the 2033 expiry of its initial term:** the rent is a
  2012 figure (no prices), and whether the extensions were taken isn't in any later source read.
- **MARTA's current ground leases:** only the 2012 briefing could be read; the article says
  "in a 2012 briefing".
- **Georgia's rules on long leases (usufruct vs estate for years) and who is taxed:** a legal
  question with tax consequences; the article tells readers to have an attorney read the lease
  and to confirm property-tax treatment with the county.
- **Typical ground-lease terms ("50 to 99 years"):** only commercial explainers say so; her
  "up to ninety-nine years" and MARTA's 99-year leases carry the point.
