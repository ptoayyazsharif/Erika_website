Researched 29 October 2026. Every fact in the article and the script is listed here with its
source and the words in it. Dated items are starred (★).

## Deliberately left out

- **The mentee's deal and fee** ($275 → $500, the landlord's offer, the client's referral): a private
  coaching matter, and agent pay isn't the topic.
- **The handbook's "more than ten" vs "fewer than ten" wording**: one sentence says the exemption is
  for a landlord who "owns fewer than ten (10) units and does not contract with a management agent";
  the rest say the duties apply to "more than ten". The article follows "more than ten units, or a
  management agent" and doesn't address exactly ten.
- **Statute numbers** (O.C.G.A. 44-7-30 ff.): the handbook doesn't cite them for deposits, and no
  official code text was read.
- **The handbook's AI tip** (paste your unsigned lease into a chatbot): interesting, but off topic.
- **Taking photos or video at move-in**: sensible, but the handbook doesn't say it; not added.
- **Commissions on tenant deals** ("a commission that comes from the landlord"): true of her
  mentee's deal; not stated as a general rule anywhere read.
