## Deliberately left out

- **How many buyers find their home online, or at an open house.** Search results gave
  52% ("found the home online", attributed to NAR) and 46% ("started online", a local
  association blog). Neither figure appears on any NAR page that could be read: the
  2025 press release and the highlights page don't state it, and the full report is
  sold, not published. Unverified, so out.
- **"Open houses have a very low incidence of violent crime… but a very high incidence of
  theft"** (NAR's *Safe Selling* guide) — only seen quoted on another page, not read in
  the guide itself.
- **What exactly the Fair Housing Act prohibits** (refusing to sell, different terms,
  discriminatory statements). HUD's overview page doesn't list it and the HUD rights page
  had no readable body. The article states only what HUD and DOJ say, and the
  not-legal-advice line sends readers to HUD or an attorney.
- **Georgia's own fair housing law.** Not read at the source; not mentioned.
- **The Smyrna listing's price, size and street** (no-prices rule, and it may have sold).
