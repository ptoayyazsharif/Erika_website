## Worked dates (derived from the rules above)

- Closed on a home any time in 2026 → you own it on January 1, 2027 → file by **April 1,
  2027** for the 2027 tax year → your base year is the **2026** assessed value (§(a)(3)(B)).
- Closed on January 2, 2027 or later → not eligible for 2027; file for 2028.

## Deliberately left out

- Tax-bill amounts, millage rates and the 40% assessment ratio. They aren't needed for
  the point and change by county and year.
- The SB 33 provision requiring voter approval for revenue growth above 3% or inflation.
  It is only in commercial explainers and the "as passed" text is hard to read for
  struck language, so it isn't stated.
- Legal advice. The article tells readers to confirm with their county.
