## Description
Erika Explains... with Erika Page. Metro Atlanta real estate, explained, with no fluff.

Each episode takes one question and explains how it works: what closing costs cover in Georgia, owning a property versus investing in one, knowing your house before you list it, cash offers, the homestead exemption, relocating to Metro Atlanta, leasing a restaurant space, and more.

Erika K. Page is a Metro Atlanta real estate agent with 24+ years in this market, licensed with Axen Realty. She works with buyers, sellers, investors and landlords across Fulton, Gwinnett, Cobb, DeKalb, Fayette and Henry counties.

Every episode has a written article, with its sources, at erikakpage.com/blog.

About the audio: episodes are voiced by an AI trained on Erika's own voice.

Explanatory only, not legal, tax or financial advice. Erika K. Page, Axen Realty. Equal Housing Opportunity.
