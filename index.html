Why Online Estimates Are Not Enough

Algorithms have never walked through your home.

Automated estimates rely on tax records and averages. They can't see your renovations, your lot, your street, or the strategy behind recent comparable sales — and they know nothing about your timeline or goals.

What Goes Into Erika's Valuation

  • True comparable sales — and the story behind each one
  • Condition, upgrades, and preparation opportunities
  • Lot, street, school zone, and micro-market position
  • Current buyer demand in your specific price band
  • Your timeline, goals, and net-proceeds targets

Your privacy is protected

Your information is used only to prepare your strategy. It is never sold, shared, or added to spam lists. Requesting a valuation creates no obligation to list.
